Yoga Classes

For calm and steadiness.

What it is

We value time to settle the mind and body, a time in which people can feel at ease.

Multilingual instructors (one man, one woman) lead yoga classes to help relieve refugees' stress: breathing techniques and basic poses that have proven very popular with a community under a lot of pressure.

Who it serves

Refugees who need stress relief and care for body and mind.

What it costs to run

REN doesn't publish a detailed cost breakdown for this program. Contact us directly for specifics.

How to support it

Through a donation, or by volunteering as an instructor.
