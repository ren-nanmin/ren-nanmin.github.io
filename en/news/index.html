Partner companies
Feb 4, 2026

PC collection with the “Omou Project”

A partnership with People Port Co., Ltd.'s "Omou Project." Conflict over the minerals used in computers has driven many people from their homes worldwide, including many of the refugees REN supports. Rather than let that hardware go to waste, we've partnered with a company that supports refugees through reuse and recycling: the buy-back value of collected devices is donated to REN. Collection dates: Feb 4, 18, 25 (Wed), Ichigaya-Tamachi, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo, 12:30–16:30.

Nov 24, 2025

We took part in the Tomokura Fes

We took part again this year in "Tomokura Fes," held on November 24 (a public holiday) at Namiki-Motomachi Park in Kawaguchi. We exhibited a wide range of African textile goods and small accessories, which many visitors bought, and our workshop was well received. We heard a lot of warm words from people who stopped by. Blessed with good weather, seven refugees joined us for a day full of smiles. Thank you to everyone who came.

Oct 8, 2025

Featured in Sekai magazine

A conversation with REN chair Mari Kobayashi appeared in issue 999 (November 2025) of Sekai magazine, published by Iwanami Shoten. In the "You and Migrants" feature, Kobayashi spoke with an essayist and a translator, both deeply engaged with refugees in Japan, under the title "Protest by Finding Joy", about everyday life alongside refugee friends.
