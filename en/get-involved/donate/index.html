Become a supporting member

A donation is a gift, in any amount and at any time. Supporting membership is an annual fee that supports REN's work year after year.

How to give by bank transfer

REN does not take card payments. Donations and membership fees are accepted by bank transfer.

Choose a donation amount
yen

¥3,000: 3 beads

An illustration only: each bead stands for ¥1,000 (up to 10 beads are shown).

Supporting membership fees are fixed (see "Two ways to support REN" above).

  1. Choose your amount

    A donation can be any amount. The annual supporting membership fee is ¥3,000 for an individual and ¥10,000 per unit for an organization.

  2. Transfer ¥3,000 to an account below

    Use your bank's app, an ATM or a counter. You can copy each detail from the card below.

  3. Write your name in the transfer

    Enter your own name as the sender. If your bank lets you add a message, please write "donation" or "membership fee".

  4. Email REN afterwards

    Send an email to refugee.empw.netwk@gmail.com with your name and the date of your transfer, so REN can thank you and confirm it.

Questions

Can I donate from abroad?

Please contact us by email and we will guide you.

Will I get a receipt?

Please contact us by email.

How is my donation used?

REN's main sources of funding are donations and membership fees. REN's main goal is to actively support refugees' economic and social independence and to protect their dignity as human beings, through cultural exchange (including the Japanese class), bead accessories and craft projects, and scholarship support.

See our activities →

Giving from outside Japan

REN also accepts donations via GoFundMe, the same route currently linked from REN's English site, which works well for international donors. Bank transfer requires a Japan-capable transfer service such as Wise.
